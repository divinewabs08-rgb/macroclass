# Kyros

Narrated chess opening lessons, as an installable offline web app:

1. **The War Council**: the foundation lesson for Black against 1.e4 (...d6, ...Nf6, ...g6), told by the High Commander and the Royal Strategist. It comes first.
2. The Pirc Defence: the Classical 6...Bg4.
3. The Pirc Defence: the Byrne Variation.
4. The Smith-Morra Gambit.

The board is green and cream by default (black and brown is the other choice), with the classic pieces. On phones and tablets the lesson opens full screen with only the board and the script.

Open the site on a phone or tablet and add it to the home screen. After the first visit it works offline.
